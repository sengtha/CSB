Demo storyline

StepWhereWhat it shows
1WalletA KYC'd citizen (Sokha) holds tokenized riel and pays another citizen — zero fees, instant finality, lost-key recovery backed by MoI.
2WalletPayments to a non-KYC'd address (Vanna) are impossible — compliance is enforced by the chain, not by apps.
3AdminSeparation of powers: MoI issues identity, the enforcement authority freezes with a court-order reference, the council governs the gateway — no institution holds two powers.
4WalletEgress: permitted tokens bridge out through the governed gateway (tier + daily cap enforced); everything else is refused.
5ExplorerThe whitelisted explorer: full sovereign auditability behind authenticated access.

Seeded accounts (keys in demo/deployments.json): Sokha — tier 2 (full KYC), Dara — tier 1 (capped), Vanna — no KYC.

Architecture at a glance

Avalanche L1 (Subnet-EVM), permissioned PoA — ministries as validators · MoI-issued on-chain KYC, no PII on chain, default one address per identity (extra slots by MoI fee) · KHRt stablecoin with a pluggable issuer · zero gas via feeManager, spam defense at the identity layer · single egress gateway wrapping Avalanche ICTT with a council-governed token allowlist, minimum tiers, daily caps and a circuit breaker · crypto-agility: upgradeable smart-account signatures for post-quantum migration. Full text: docs/architecture.md.
