Access is self-service

Any KYC-active address activates its own scoped RPC URL at rpc-access.html by signing with its wallet — no per-user issuing, no admin step. Access is re-checked live on every request against on-chain KYC and the revoke list, so revoking KYC also cuts RPC. Use the tools here only to revoke an address, or to fetch a URL for someone who can't self-serve.
