what an authority says the rate is, beside what the market did

What this is

Two price sources with the same interface. One reports an administered rate — a figure a publishing role sets, within a bound, with a citation attached. The other reads a market rate straight out of this chain's own Uniswap pool, with no publisher and no off-chain infrastructure at all.

The point of having both is the gap between them. On a sovereign chain, the divergence between an official rate and a traded rate is the monetary question itself — and here it can be read off the ledger instead of surveyed.

Publish a rate

Only the publishing role can do this, and the contract will not accept a figure without a source reference — an administered number with no stated source is indistinguishable from an invented one. Moves larger than the configured deviation bound are refused outright.

Two things worth understanding before trusting either number

The administered oracle fails closed. If nobody republishes within its max age, it stops answering — and if it is wired into the lending market, every Aave read reverts: supply, borrow, withdraw, liquidate, and the health-factor display. That is the right behaviour for a credit market running on a rate nobody is standing behind, and it is also a way to halt the market by forgetting. The liveness of the market becomes a duty of the publisher.
The TWAP is a measurement instrument here, not a valuation source. A time-weighted price costs as much to manipulate as the liquidity behind it, and this pool is small. A longer window raises the attack cost and makes the price staler; neither end of that trade is safe at this size. Nothing points the lending market at it, deliberately.
And it is not a feed. The pair accumulates the price by itself, but somebody must read the accumulator and divide, or there is no average to quote. The update button above is permissionless — anyone may call it, which is what makes the price trustless — but "anyone can" is not "someone does".

An honest caveat about the divergence: the pool trades KHRt against a plain test token that nobody else trades, so the market rate is currently whatever the pool was seeded at. It becomes a real measurement when something other than this experiment moves that pool.
