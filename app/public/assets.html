Swap — KHRt ⇄ tRIEL

The two tokens above are the same money in two forms: tRIEL is the native coin that pays gas and settles between institutions; KHRt is a tokenized riel issued by a government-authorized issuer. The converter swaps between them at a fixed 1:1 — no rate, no spread, no fee beyond gas.

Wrapping locks KHRt in the converter and mints you the same amount of tRIEL. Unwrapping burns tRIEL and releases the KHRt that was backing it. So every tRIEL the converter has ever minted is matched by KHRt sitting in it — a figure you can check yourself, below, rather than take on trust.

Mint a collectible

A KYC-verified address can mint one for itself. The artwork is generated on chain — no image server, no IPFS, nothing to go offline.

Minting is a real transaction on a permissioned chain, so it needs three things: an active KYC attestation, permission to transact (the chain's allow list), and a little tRIEL for gas. If any is missing you will be told which — that refusal is the demonstration, not a fault.
