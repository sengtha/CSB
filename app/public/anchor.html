🌳 Anchor a garden record

sign it with your own wallet — the record stays on your phone

Your phone recorded and signed a plant in your garden. This page commits its hash to CSB, so the date stops being your phone's word for it and a licensed field verifier has somewhere to put their name.

Only hashes travel. Your photo, your coordinates and your device key never leave your phone, and the plot's name is never sent as text. The name's hash is public on the chain, though, and a short everyday name — home-garden-01 — can be worked back out of that hash by anyone willing to spend a minute on it. Until we fix that properly, pick a plot name you would not mind a stranger guessing. You sign with your own wallet; nobody here can send it for you.

1 · Paste the calldata from your garden app

In iAny, open /garden → Anchor on CSB → Copy. Or follow the “Sign on CSB” link, which fills this in for you.

2 · Check you are allowed to anchor

Three separate gates, and they fail in ways that look nothing alike. Better to read them here than to watch a transaction revert.

3 · Sign it

Gas on CSB is free, so anchoring costs a signature and nothing else.

Anchoring is not verification. It records that this exact record existed by this date, and nothing more. Whether the plants are really there is a separate question, answered by a licensed field verifier going to look — which is the only thing that lets a title be issued or a sponsor's money move. CSB records trees, not carbon; nothing here is a carbon credit.

Illustrative demonstration on a test network. “Licensed field verifier” and every other institutional role here is a hypothetical placeholder. No real grove, licence or programme is represented.
