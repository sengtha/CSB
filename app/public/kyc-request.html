CSB is permissioned: an address can hold tokenized riel and transact only once an identity authority has registered it. This page is how you ask. It is free, sends no transaction, and grants nothing by itself — an authority reviews the queue and decides.

1
Connect your wallet, or paste an address you control.
2
Sign a message proving the address is yours. Signing is free and moves nothing.
3
An authority approves or refuses it. Come back here to see the status.

What reaches the chain, and what does not. If this is approved, the chain records a commitment hash and a tier against your address — never your name, and never anything typed above. That is the same rule the rest of CSB follows: the ledger proves an address was verified, without publishing who anyone is.

Test network. Verification here is a demonstration of the mechanism and carries no real-world status of any kind. The reviewing "identity authority" is a placeholder role held by the operator of this prototype, not any institution.
