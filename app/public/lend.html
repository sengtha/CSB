Supply & borrow

Supplying gives you aKHRt, which grows as borrowers pay interest. Borrowing against your own supply is possible here because collateral and debt are the same asset — a real market would have several.

Collateral is per-address. The Supplied figure in the market card above is the pool's total across everyone; the one in Your position is yours. Aave lends only against your own, so supplying from one wallet and borrowing from another is refused — with code 34.

What this demonstrates about the perimeter

Compliance holds where the asset moves. Withdrawing KHRt to an address without a KYC attestation reverts, exactly as a direct transfer would.
It does not hold on the receipt. aKHRt is a plain ERC-20 with no compliance hooks, so a supplier can hand it to an address that could not receive one riel. Unlike a liquidity-pool share, it then grows — the holder's exposure increases with no further transaction by anyone.
Liquidation is permissionless in Aave — anyone may repay a bad debt and take the collateral. An unverified liquidator is blocked here, but not by CSB's governance: repaying means paying KHRt, and an unattested address cannot hold KHRt to pay with. That is a protection nobody designed, and it would disappear the moment the debt asset was something ungated.
