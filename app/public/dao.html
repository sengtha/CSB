🇰🇭 DAOs

decide things together, weighted by a token

A DAO here is a group that decides things by vote, weighted by whatever token it chose — KHRt, a pool's LP token, a staking token, anything on this chain. Anyone can create one, anyone holding the token can propose, and the count is arithmetic on the ledger that anybody can recheck.

Creating a DAO here is not a contract deployment — it is a record in one shared contract. That is why it takes seconds and needs no special permission, where deploying a governance contract on CSB would need a vetted deployer.

A closed proposal is a recorded decision, not a transaction. Nothing executes automatically — acting on an outcome is a job for a multisig whose owners can read the result. Keeping the counting apart from the spending means a bug in one cannot move the other.
